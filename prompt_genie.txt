# Objective

I have an existing `SCENARIOS.json` containing **5,300 curated healthcare user scenarios** mapped to **53 training rules**. Each scenario represents a unique clinical intent and is associated with a rule, category, priority, style, and user query.

My goal is to expand this dataset to approximately **200,000 high-quality, clinically realistic user scenarios** for fine-tuning a healthcare Small Language Model (SLM).

The expansion **must preserve the original clinical intent** of every scenario. The objective is **not** to paraphrase randomly, but to systematically increase diversity while remaining faithful to the associated training rule.

Examples:

## Requirements

1. Preserve the original rule_id.
2. Preserve the original clinical intent.
3. Never introduce unsafe or contradictory medical advice.
4. Produce natural language that sounds like a real patient.
5. Avoid duplicates.
6. Maintain balanced distribution across all rules.
7. Maintain balanced distribution across personas and emotions.
8. Keep language varied while remaining clinically accurate.

## Expected Output

Do NOT generate all scenarios yet.

Instead, design a complete scenario expansion strategy.

Return:

1. Recommended augmentation architecture.
2. Number of variants per scenario.
3. Distribution across personas.
4. Distribution across emotions.
5. Distribution across contexts.
6. Distribution across conversation depth.
7. Estimated final dataset size.
8. Rules to prevent duplicate or low-quality scenarios.
9. Recommended JSON schema for the expanded scenarios.
10. A scalable strategy that can later generate approximately 200,000 scenarios while maintaining high quality.

Think like an AI Dataset Architect rather than a chatbot.
